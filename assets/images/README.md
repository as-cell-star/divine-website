# Divine Birth — Image Policy & Photo Shot-List

## Rule
**Every photograph on this site is a real photograph taken at Divine Birth.**
No stock photography. No AI-generated images. A maternity clinic's
credibility depends on patients being able to trust what they see.

Services we cannot yet illustrate with a real photo are presented in the
**"Also available at the centre"** index (icons + text), not with a
mismatched or stock image. That is a deliberate design choice, and it
looks intentional rather than misleading.

## Deliberately NOT used

- `hero1.jpg` — this is the **brochure/flyer artwork**, not a photograph.
  It was previously the first hero slide and the social-share image, which
  meant the first thing every visitor saw was a poster of the site they
  were already on. Removed.
- `service-team.jpg`, `service-handover.jpg`, `service-birth.jpg` —
  **graphic clinical/surgical photographs** (gloves, drapes, cord clamping).
  These were being shown on public service cards and in the gallery.
  They are distressing for an expectant mother browsing the site and are
  not an appropriate first impression. Removed from public view.
  Keep them for clinical/training use if useful.

## Shot-list: photos to take (then drop straight in)

Take these on any decent phone, in landscape, with **written patient
consent**. Once you have them, save with the filename below and the site
will pick them up with no code changes needed — I have left the slots
ready.

| Filename to save as | Shot needed |
|---|---|
| `photo-antenatal.jpg` | A midwife doing an antenatal check-up — measuring fundal height, or listening to the fetal heartbeat with a doppler. |
| `photo-ultrasound.jpg` | The ultrasound machine in use, probe on the abdomen, screen visible. |
| `photo-lab.jpg` | The laboratory bench — samples, microscope, analyser. No patient faces needed. |
| `photo-class.jpg` | An antenatal class in session — several mothers, birthing balls, midwife teaching. |
| `photo-family-planning.jpg` | A private counselling consultation — midwife and patient talking across a desk. Faces can be turned away. |
| `photo-exterior.jpg` | The front of the building at Kahawa Wendani, so patients can recognise it when they arrive. |

**Consent matters.** Get it in writing, and never publish a patient's face
without explicit permission for public/website use. When in doubt, shoot
from behind or crop out faces — a photo of hands, equipment, or a room is
still infinitely better than a stock image.

Once you send me any of these, I will promote those services from the
text index up into full photo cards.
